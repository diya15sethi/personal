08 · WHAT BEAUTY MEANS TO ME

Beauty is what remains
when the mirror is put down.

People often say that true beauty lies in the eyes of the beholder, or that beauty is purely internal. I disagree with both. A person's beauty cannot be faked, and it cannot be hidden for long. Your identity and your individuality are your own and beauty, to me, is simply how comfortable you are living inside them. It shows up in the smaller things. In how you speak to someone much younger or much older than you. In how you treat people who are entirely different from you. In how you show up for the things that matter, in whatever way you can — even if it isn't loud. Beauty is not what you look like. It is what people quietly feel around you.
